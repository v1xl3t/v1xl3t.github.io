RésuméStudio

A no-install résumé editor. Your data stays in your browser, nothing is uploaded.

  1. Click any text to edit it, fields highlight on hover and edits autosave locally.
  2. Library holds every job, project and bullet you have, tagged by skill and industry.
  3. Include is a checklist. Filter by tag, tick what fits the job, the résumé rebuilds.
  4. Variants saves and duplicates whole résumés, one per role you are chasing.
  5. Print / PDF, Export, Link and QR all respect what you ticked.
