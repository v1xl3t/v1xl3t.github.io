Three ways to move through this

Same work, same filter, three different answers to where the controls live. Each is built and working, so you can judge them by using them rather than by reading about them. Try each one with a thumb.

Option A Rail

A quiet column of markers down the edge, and the type filter sits inline with the work.

Works because Reads as the least interface. Nothing floats over the work.

Costs you Twenty-two markers is a tall column on a phone, and the filter is only where you left it.

Option B Dock

A bar docked at the bottom that collapses to where you are, and opens upward into the filter.

Works because The filter is always one thumb away, in the part of a phone a thumb reaches.

Costs you It is the only option with something permanently on screen.

Option C Drawer

A tab on the right edge pulls a full panel in, with the filter and the whole list inside.

Works because Nothing covers the work until you ask, and the panel has room to be generous.

Costs you An edge tab is the least obvious of the three, so it needs a nudge on a first visit.
