What this actually does, and what it does not

  • It finds drum hits with spectral flux onset detection, then sorts them into kick, snare and hi hat by where their energy sits in the spectrum. That is classic signal processing, not machine learning, so there is no model to download and the analysis takes a couple of seconds.
  • Toms and cymbals are not detected as such. They land in the nearest of the three lanes and usually come through with low confidence. Notes the detector was unsure about are drawn with a dashed outline, and the editor is there so you can fix them.
  • It does not separate the drums from the rest of the mix. The centre cut in the practice tools is a rough stand in. Real separation means tens of megabytes of model weights, which is a fair upgrade one day but not something to make you wait for now.
  • Your file never leaves this device. There is no account, no upload, no analytics.
