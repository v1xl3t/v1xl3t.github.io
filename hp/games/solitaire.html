How to play
  • Build the four foundations up from the ace to the king, one suit each.
  • Build the seven columns down, always alternating red and black.
  • Only a king can move into an empty column.
  • Tap the deck to turn cards over. When it runs out, tap it again to turn the pile back over.
  • Drag a card, or tap it once to pick it up and tap where you want it. Tap the same card twice to send it straight to its foundation.
  • Undo walks back through your last eighty moves. Your game is saved in this browser, so closing the tab does not lose it.
  • Hint lights one move you can still make. When there is genuinely nothing left, the game says so rather than letting you hunt.
