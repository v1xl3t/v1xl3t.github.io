How to play
  • Clear all thirty five cards off the seven columns.
  • Only the card at the bottom of a column can be played, and it can only go on the pile if it is one rank above or below the card showing there.
  • Tap the deck when nothing fits, to turn the next card over. There are sixteen in it and no second pass.
  • By default the king is a wall. Nothing follows a king, and that is where most lost deals come from. Switch to wrapping and the ace and the king join up, which makes a much kinder game.
  • Tap a card that fits to play it, or drag it onto the pile. A card that can be played is ringed, so you are never counting ranks in your head.
  • Undo walks back through your last eighty moves. Your game is saved in this browser, so closing the tab does not lose it.
