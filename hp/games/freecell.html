Race the same deal

One of you starts a race and reads out the code, the other types it in. You both get the identical deal and play your own board, so nobody is waiting on anybody. First to clear it wins. Starting a race deals a new board.

How to play
  • Build the four foundations up from the ace to the king, one suit each.
  • Build the eight columns down, always alternating red and black.
  • The four cells on the left hold one card each. Anything can go in one, and getting it back out is your problem.
  • Any card can move into an empty column, not just a king.
  • You are only ever moving one card. Moving a run is the game staging it through your free cells, so the number it will carry is the free cells plus one, doubled for every empty column. Fill all four cells and you can move exactly one card.
  • Every card is face up from the first second, so there is nothing to discover and no luck left after the deal.
  • Drag a card, or tap it once to pick it up and tap where you want it. Tap the same card twice to send it straight to its foundation.
  • Undo walks back through your last eighty moves. Your game is saved in this browser, so closing the tab does not lose it.
  • Hint lights one move you can still make. When a deal genuinely cannot reach a foundation any more, the game says so rather than letting you hunt.
