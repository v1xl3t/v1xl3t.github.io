How to play
  • Take the whole pyramid apart. Every pair of cards that adds up to thirteen comes off together.
  • An ace is one, a jack is eleven, a queen is twelve, and a king is thirteen on its own, so a king leaves by itself.
  • A card can only be taken once both of the cards resting on it have gone. The bottom row is free from the start.
  • The card showing next to the deck counts as free too, so it can pair with anything on the pyramid.
  • Tap one card, then tap the other. Tap the same card again to put it back down.
  • Three passes through the deck is the friendly game. One pass is the hard one, and it decides whether a deal was ever winnable, so changing it starts a new game.
  • Undo walks back through your last eighty moves. Your game is saved in this browser, so closing the tab does not lose it.
