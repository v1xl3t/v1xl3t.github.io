Race the same deal

One of you starts a race and reads out the code, the other types it in. You both get the identical deal and play your own board, so nobody is waiting on anybody. First to clear it wins. Starting a race deals a new board.

How to play
  • Two decks, ten columns, and eight runs to finish. A run is a king down to an ace all in one suit, and it leaves the board the moment it is complete.
  • Build downward on any card one rank higher. Suit does not matter for where a card may land.
  • Suit does matter for what travels together. Cards move as a group only when they are already one suit in descending order, so a nine of hearts on a ten of spades is a legal place to put it and not a group you can pick up later.
  • Any card can go into an empty column, and an empty column is the most valuable thing on the board.
  • Tap the deck to deal one card onto every column. There are five rows of ten, and you cannot deal at all while any column is empty, which is the rule that makes this game hard.
  • One suit is a puzzle you can learn. Four is a fight. Two is where most people live. Changing it starts a new game, because the suit count is the difficulty.
  • Drag a card, or tap it once to pick it up and tap the column you want. Undo walks back through your last eighty moves, including the row you wish you had not dealt.
